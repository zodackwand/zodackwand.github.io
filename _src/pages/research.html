ANN search under distribution shift

Supervised by Prof. Hakan Ferhatosmanoglu.

Graph-based nearest-neighbour indices such as HNSW are constructed over the base data. Every decision the construction makes — which neighbours to keep, which long edges justify their memory — is a bet that future queries resemble that data. Queries need not oblige. I am characterising what the mismatch costs, when the loss is a property of the graph rather than of where the search begins, and which corrections are cheap enough to apply online.

Status: experiments running.

One-step generative models for translation between signals

Supervised by Prof. Yu Guan.

Flow matching methods that learn an average velocity field, in the manner of MeanFlow, admit generation in a single step rather than dozens. I am looking at what the averaging costs when source and target domains are structurally different, and where the one-step approximation stops being defensible.

Status: early.
