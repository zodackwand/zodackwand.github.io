I study Computer Engineering and Mathematics at the University of Warwick. I work on efficient inference, hardware-aware algorithm design, and the theory of deep learning. Before that I interned at startups, writing backend systems for LLM pipelines.

Current work#

With Prof. Hakan Ferhatosmanoglu, on nearest-neighbour search under distribution shift. Graph indices such as HNSW are built over the base data, but the queries that arrive afterwards need not come from the same distribution. I am measuring what that mismatch costs and what can be done about it cheaply.

With Prof. Yu Guan, on one-step generative models for translation between signals. The approach follows flow matching methods such as MeanFlow, which learn average velocity fields rather than instantaneous ones and so allow generation in a single step.
