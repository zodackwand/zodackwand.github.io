Retrieval under distribution shift

Graph-based nearest-neighbour indices assume queries are drawn from the distribution the index was built over. When they are not, recall degrades gradually rather than visibly. I am characterising when that happens, how much of it is a starting-point problem rather than a graph problem, and what the cheapest correction is.

Status: experiments running. Draft in September.  ·  Related note

Few-step generation between signal domains

Flow-matching methods that learn an average velocity field admit sampling in one or two steps instead of dozens. I am looking at what the averaging costs when the source and target domains are structurally different, and where the one-step approximation stops being defensible.

Status: early. Nothing worth citing yet.

Napkin math for inference

An open collection of the back-of-envelope calculations that decide whether an optimisation is worth attempting: bytes moved per token, arithmetic intensity per operator, the ratio between the roofline and what we actually observe. Written up as short, checkable derivations rather than benchmarks.
