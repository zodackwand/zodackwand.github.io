Roman Bikbulatov

I study Computer Engineering and Mathematics at the University of Warwick.

My research interests are efficient inference, hardware-aware algorithm design, and the theory of deep learning.

Previously I interned at startups, working on backend systems for LLM-based pipelines.

Current work

ANN search under distribution shift. With Prof. Hakan Ferhatosmanoglu. Graph indices such as HNSW are built over the base data, but queries need not come from the same distribution; I work on what that mismatch costs and how to handle it.

One-step generative models for translation between signals. With Prof. Yu Guan. The approach follows flow matching methods such as MeanFlow, which learn average velocity fields and so admit generation in a single step.
