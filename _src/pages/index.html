I study Computer Engineering and Mathematics at the University of Warwick. Before that, I was at UC Irvine for a year, studying Math & CS.

I'm interested in efficient inference, hardware-aware algorithm design, and the theory of deep learning. Alongside my studies, I've interned at startups, working on backend systems.

Current work

With Prof. Hakan Ferhatosmanoglu, on nearest-neighbour search under distribution shift. Graph indices such as HNSW are built over the base data, but the queries that arrive afterwards need not come from the same distribution. I'm working on efficient ways to handle such queries.

With Prof. Yu Guan, on one-step generative models for translation between signals. The approach follows flow matching methods such as MeanFlow.
