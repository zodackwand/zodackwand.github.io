Research

Nearest-neighbour search under distribution shift#

Supervised by Prof. Hakan Ferhatosmanoglu.

Graph indices such as HNSW are constructed over the base data. Which neighbours a node keeps, and which long edges are worth their memory, are decided once, against whatever vectors are there at build time. Queries arrive later and need not resemble them. I am measuring what the mismatch costs, separating the part of the loss that belongs to the graph from the part that belongs to where the search begins, and looking for corrections cheap enough to apply online.

Experiments are running. There is no draft yet.

One-step generative models for translation between signals#

Supervised by Prof. Yu Guan.

Flow matching methods that learn an average velocity field, as MeanFlow does, allow generation in one step instead of dozens. I am looking at what the averaging costs when the source and target domains differ in structure, and at where the one-step approximation stops holding.

This is at an early stage.

Interests#

Efficient inference, hardware-aware algorithm design, and the theory of deep learning. In practice most of the work is measurement: deciding which number to trust, and which improvement is worth the week it would take.
